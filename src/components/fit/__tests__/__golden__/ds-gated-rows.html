Turning it on

Matching is always computed — this flag only controls who can see it. Set BLUEPRINT_MATCHING_ENABLED=true on the blueprint-service Lambda via CDK (-c blueprintMatching=true), not by editing the Lambda directly — a direct edit is reverted by the next deploy. Full steps in docs/runbooks/enable_role_matching.md.

This gates Job Matches, and Job Matches gates My Plan and Interview Prep — so all three come back together.
