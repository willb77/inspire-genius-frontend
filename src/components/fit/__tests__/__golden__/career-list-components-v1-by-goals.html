What Job Fit is for

Job Fit compares how you're wired against what a role actually demands, so you can see the difference and act on it. A fit score is a comparison, not a verdict — a low one usually means a specific, nameable gap, and naming it is the point.

  1. 1Blueprint a role

    Upload a posting or describe one. The Job DNA engine drafts a behavioral benchmark from it — a starting point a person reviews, not a validated PRISM benchmark — and saves the role for reuse.

  2. 2See the fit

    Your PRISM profile against that benchmark, dimension by dimension — where you line up, where you don't, and by how much.

  3. 3Do something about it

    Coaching turns the gaps into a conversation: what to close, what to work around, and how to talk about it in an interview.
