What this is

Career Pathways looks beyond the roles you already match closely and highlights the ones you're developing toward — roles a step or two out where your profile is a workable-but-growing fit. For each, it shows the specific behaviors to build; the skill ladders break those into small, concrete steps.

  • Adjacent roles — growth targets ranked by how reachable they are from where you are now.
  • Roadmaps — make a role your target and the growth areas between your profile and its blueprint become the steps, in the order to work on them.

Pathways are personalized to your PRISM profile and the roles published to you. As more roles are published — or as you complete your assessment — more pathways appear here.
